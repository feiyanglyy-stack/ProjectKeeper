# Code of Conduct

This project follows the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**. Its full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/> and applies here as written.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise the work and not the person, and accept that maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that go against this.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here has been unacceptable, write to the maintainer at <angelina2026999@gmail.com>. The address is read daily; what you report is read by the maintainers only and kept confidential. You will get an answer.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what follows a report, along the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent one.
